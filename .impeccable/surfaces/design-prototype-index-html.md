# Landing page (single page)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a Hadera resident under stress, usually on a phone. Action: WhatsApp or call.
Proof available: 30 years, personal handling, address, portrait. No testimonials, ratings or case counts.
Palette brief from the owner: seriousness, warmth, care. Motion brief: high-end scroll-driven motion (reference: "Baseline" tennis club build).

## Direction contract
THESIS: A personal lawyer, not a firm. Refuses the category default of stock courthouse imagery, scales of justice and a templated corporate grid; the navy and gold palette (owner's choice) is carried by the personal, hand-made details instead.
OWN-WORLD: Approved palette cleangold: navy ink #14233A and #1F3452 (seriousness), gold #CDAE6A with #7A5B18 for text on light (warmth), clean white #F8F7F4 and #FFFFFF (care). Frank Ruhl Libre display, Assistant body. Ruled legal-pad hairlines on dark fields, a notary seal as the brand mark, court-file folders with tabs.
STORY: Visitor sees Yossi's face and "30 years in Hadera", reads that he handles the case personally, finds their area in a folder, and sends a pre-filled WhatsApp message.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark inset card. Right: two-line serif headline (second line brass), one paragraph, WhatsApp pill + phone. Left: arched portrait with a rotating notary seal overlapping its corner. Header with name, 4 links, phone and WhatsApp.
FORM: Scroll-told single page; signature interactions: seal rotates with scroll and stamps at the close, statement words light up as you read, "30" fills with brass, folders stack sticky, signature draws. Seed key a0011b2a (degraded roll, owner-pinned palette brief).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Open decisions
Voice (first or third person), sub-services per area, hours, parking, education, real signature, high-res portrait, office photos, logo.
